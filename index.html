Sat / Sun

5K + Recovery

Run days

Run Saturday easy, Sunday harder — or the reverse. Two days off lifting is what lets four upper-body sessions a week actually work.

What changed

Your old plan hit chest, back and arms once a week each. This one hits them twice, which is where most of the growth comes from. Legs drop from two sessions to one, and Friday's barbell deadlifts are gone so they don't wreck your Saturday run.

Progressive overload

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add 2.5–5 lbs next week. Log every set — the plan only works if the numbers move.

Keeping it under an hour

Rest times are on each lift. Stick to them, use the supersets, and set up the second exercise before you start the first. If you're running long, cut the last isolation movement.

Effort

Leave 1–2 reps in the tank on your first sets and take the last set of each isolation lift close to failure. Compounds don't need to be grinders every week.

Deload

Every 7–8 weeks, cut your sets in half for one week and keep the weight the same. Your joints and your runs will both thank you.

Fuel

0.8–1g of protein per lb of bodyweight. With 10 km of running on top of five lifting days, a slight surplus is what makes the difference between maintaining and growing.
